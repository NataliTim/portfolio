05 · marketing and web

Supporting the Business Beyond the Product

Delivered the surrounding layer the business needed to promote and sell its products — company websites, landing pages, video and presentations — instrumented with Google Analytics and Tag Manager and integrated with the sales CRM.

The product was only part of what the business needed to sell. Alongside it sat the material buyers actually met first: the company websites, the campaign landing pages, the video, and the presentations used in sales conversations. That material was delivered as one stream of work rather than a set of one-off requests.

Because the same stream also owned intake, measurement and the CRM handoff, a request could be raised, produced, published, tracked and followed up without changing hands.

After the rebranding, worked out with the marketing team and external consultants, the company's websites were rebuilt to the new brand guidelines and brought them up to date. Each one carried the same landing-page structure underneath, so a new campaign meant new copy and assets rather than a new page design, and the same form and tracking sat in the same place every time.

The rebuild turned the websites into a lead source: enquiries started arriving through them and landing in the sales CRM.

Finding the current version of a document used to mean digging through the company archives, and the file that turned up was often out of date. The document templates were produced and placed where the work happens: Word, PowerPoint and Excel, available in one click.

Alongside them, the wider material was standardised to the new brand after the rebranding: marketing collaterals, social media templates, emails and landing pages, plus email signatures, business cards and many more. The approved assets sat in shared libraries, so everyone drew from the same source.
