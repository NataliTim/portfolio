Rebuilding automotive accessories e-commerce

I redesigned an automotive-accessories platform integrated with manufacturer and dealer websites, reworking search and the customer flow through testing with real users.

Omni-search, fewer steps, and important information brought into view.

E-commerce Full redesign Integrations User research
overview decisions integration outcomes

The platform brought automotive accessories into manufacturer and dealer website experiences. The work combined a full interface redesign with integration constraints. A related tire-sales workflow served both dealership staff and online customers.

Platform audiences and research participants are kept separate here: dealership staff and online customers are documented as audiences of the tire workflow, not as the participants of the redesign study.

The redesign focused on three connected problems: search, unnecessary steps, and information that was not visible when users needed it. I reworked the flow around omni-search, reduced the steps, and brought hidden information into the open, then tested the changes with real users.
